Children's swimming lessons in Essex

Fun, friendly group lessons for children aged 3 and up, taught in small groups so every child gets plenty of practice.

Aqua Heroes Swim School teaches children's swimming lessons in Hornchurch, Brentwood, Basildon, Billericay and Loughton. Children can join from age 3, in groups of 3 to 4 beginners per teacher, for 25 minute lessons from £12.50 each.

How children's lessons work

  • Lessons last 25 minutes, in a 30 minute slot.
  • Groups have 3 to 4 beginners per teacher, depending on age. From stage 5, groups have 5 to 6 learners, depending on ability.
  • Lessons follow the Swim England Learn to Swim Pathway: stages 1 to 10, with Ducklings for pre-schoolers.
  • Termly recognition and awards, with official Swim England certificates and badges included.
  • Online progress tracking through our secure learner portal.

Prices

Group lessons start from £12.50 per 25 minute lesson. The trial lesson is free of charge if you go on to book a 9 week term (T&Cs apply). Prices depend on the pool and time slot, so contact us for exact prices and any current offers.

Where and when

  • Hornchurch, The Campion School: Saturday, 3 to 7 pm, and Sunday, 7.30 to 11.25 am.
  • Brentwood, St Helens Junior School: Wednesday, 4.15 to 6.45 pm.
  • Basildon, James Hornsby School: Monday and Wednesday, 7 to 9 pm, and Sunday, 1 to 3 pm.
  • Billericay, Quilters Junior School: Friday, 3.45 to 5.45 pm (child groups and 1:1).
  • Loughton, Davenant Foundation School: Thursday, 7.15 to 9.15 pm.

Teaching slots are allocated by ability level and can change. See all pools and times.

Questions about Children's lessons

What age can my child start group lessons?

Group lessons are for children aged 3 and up. For babies and pre-schoolers, see our baby and pre-school lessons.

How many children are in each class?

Regular group lessons have 3 to 4 beginners per teacher, depending on age. Stages 5 and above have 5 to 6 learners, depending on ability.

How long is a lesson?

Each lesson is 25 minutes, in a 30 minute slot.

Do children get certificates and badges?

Yes. We give termly recognition and awards, with official Swim England certificates and badges all included.

How do I book?

Complete the registration form on our booking page. We reply with current availability and any price offers, then arrange a trial lesson at a time that suits you.

Ready to get in the water?

Register for a free, no obligation consultation. We will confirm availability and arrange a trial lesson.
