Private swimming lessons in Essex

One-to-one and one-to-two lessons shaped around your learner, including lessons for children with SEN.

Aqua Heroes Swim School offers private 1:1 and 1:2 swimming lessons, including SEN 1:1 lessons, for babies, children and adults. Lessons are 25 minutes in a 30 minute slot and start from £35 for 1:1 and £40 for 1:2.

Lesson options

  • 1:1 lessons with a teacher to yourself, tailored to your needs.
  • 1:2 lessons for related learners of a similar ability level.
  • SEN 1:1 and SEN group lessons at selected pools.
  • Lessons at your own indoor pool at home, if you have one.
  • Availability across all of our locations, subject to availability.

Questions about Private 1:1 and 1:2

How much do private swimming lessons cost?

1:1 lessons start from £35 per 25 minute lesson (30 minute slot) for the trial and for term block bookings, or £40 pay as you go. 1:2 lessons start from £40, or £45 pay as you go.

What is a 1:2 lesson?

Two related learners of a similar ability level share one lesson with a teacher.

Do you teach children with SEN?

Yes. We offer SEN 1:1 lessons and SEN group lessons at selected pools. Contact us to talk through your child's needs and we will suggest the best option.

Can you teach at my own pool?

Yes. If you have your own indoor pool, we also offer lessons at your home. Contact us to discuss your location and needs.

How do I book?

Complete the registration form on our booking page. We reply with current availability and any price offers, then arrange a trial lesson at a time that suits you.

Ready to get in the water?

Register for a free, no obligation consultation. We will confirm availability and arrange a trial lesson.
