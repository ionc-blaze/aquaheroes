About us

Our mission is to create the next generation of swimming lovers, and perhaps inspire a few future Olympians along the way.

History and mission

Aqua Heroes' lead teacher and founder began his swimming journey young, teaching himself to swim at a local gym with no formal instruction.

After several years as a highly qualified and experienced teacher with Water Babies Essex, he set out on his own and founded Aqua Heroes. The school has grown organically from a small handful of clients he taught from babies, and through word of mouth.

Our priority is that every client leaves the pool happy and excited to return, so we hold ourselves to the highest quality of service in everything we do.

Want to know more about our lessons? Take a look at the FAQs or get in touch.

How we teach

  • Small groups, so every learner gets plenty of practice and attention.
  • A recognition and reward programme built on the Swim England Learn to Swim Pathway.
  • Qualified, experienced teachers who keep lessons fun.
  • Lessons for ages 3 months and up, together with our early years sister school, Little Legends.
