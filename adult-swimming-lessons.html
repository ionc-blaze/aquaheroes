Adult swimming lessons in Essex

Whether you are a complete beginner or just need some tips to improve your technique, we have the right approach for you.

Aqua Heroes Swim School teaches adults to swim and to improve their technique. Adult group lessons run on Thursday evenings, 8.30 to 10 pm, in Hornchurch, adult lessons run in the daytime in Billericay, and private 1:1 lessons are available across our locations.

Lessons for every level

  • Complete beginners learning to swim for the first time.
  • Confident swimmers who want to improve their technique.
  • Adult group lessons, or private 1:1 lessons tailored to you.

Where and when

  • Hornchurch, The Campion School: Thursday, 8.30 to 10 pm (adult group lessons).
  • Billericay, Quilters Junior School: Daytime lessons on Tuesday, Thursday and Friday.

Prices

Private 1:1 lessons start from £35 per 25 minute lesson (30 minute slot) when you book a term, or £40 pay as you go. Contact us for adult group lesson prices and current offers.

Questions about Adult lessons

Can complete beginners have adult lessons?

Yes. Whether you are a complete beginner or just need some tips to improve your technique, we have the right approach for you.

Do you offer adult group lessons?

Yes. Adult group lessons run on Thursday evenings from 8.30 to 10 pm in Hornchurch, and Billericay offers adult lessons in the daytime.

Can I have private adult lessons?

Yes. 1:1 lessons are available across our locations, subject to availability, and start from £35 per 25 minute lesson when you book a term.

How do I book?

Complete the registration form on our booking page. We reply with current availability and any price offers, then arrange a trial lesson at a time that suits you.

Ready to get in the water?

Register for a free, no obligation consultation. We will confirm availability and arrange a trial lesson.
