Swimming lessons for every age

Pick the lessons that suit your learner, from parent and baby sessions to adult group lessons and holiday crash courses.

Holiday crash courses

Non-term time crash courses and other sessions are available during half-term holidays, including Christmas, Easter and summer. Crash courses can be 1:1, 1:2 or child group lessons.

Price depends on your requirements and is subject to availability. Register on our booking page and tell us what you are looking for.

How terms and trials work

  • Terms are 9 weeks long.
  • The trial lesson for group lessons is free of charge if you go on to book a term (T&Cs apply).
  • 1:1 and 1:2 lessons are discounted when you book a term (T&Cs apply).
  • Lessons run all year round, except Christmas, Easter and 1 to 2 weeks in the summer while our pools close for planned maintenance.

Common questions

What types of swimming lessons do you offer?

We offer group lessons, private 1:1 and 1:2 lessons including SEN, baby and pre-school lessons, adult lessons, and holiday crash courses.

How long is a term?

Terms are 9 weeks. The trial lesson for group lessons is free of charge if you go on to book a term (T&Cs apply).

Do you run lessons in the school holidays?

Yes. Non-term time crash courses and other sessions are available in half-term holidays, including Christmas, Easter and summer. Price depends on your requirements and availability.

How do I book?

Complete the registration form on our booking page. We reply with current availability and any price offers, then arrange a trial lesson at a time that suits you.

Ready to get in the water?

Register for a free, no obligation consultation. We will confirm availability and arrange a trial lesson.
