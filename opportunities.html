Work with us

We are a modest team that aims to make a huge positive impact in everything we do.

Join our team

If you are passionate about swimming and care about delivering the highest level of service while teaching others to swim, we would love to hear from you.

Our bespoke Learn to Swim programme is high energy, positive and a lot of fun. We offer a supportive team environment, teacher onboarding, flexible and regular weekly hours and fantastic rates of pay.

Who we are looking for

  • Energetic people who love working with children of all ages.
  • A relevant Level 1 or 2 swimming teacher qualification.
  • A current National Rescue or NPLQ qualification.

This organisation is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people, and expects all staff and volunteers to share this commitment.
