Book a lesson

We embrace the FUNdamentals of learning to swim, so our swimmers always leave the pool smiling and happy.

How booking works

  1. Register

    Complete our no obligation registration form so we have your full details. It is also the way to ask about prices, 1:1 and 1:2 lessons including SEN 1:1s, or a private consultation.

  2. We confirm availability

    We reply with current availability and any price offers, and add you to a waiting list if your preferred slot is full.

  3. Start with a trial

    We book you in for a trial lesson at a time that suits you. The trial is free when you then book a 9 week term (T&Cs apply).

Complete the registration form Message us on WhatsApp

The form opens in a new tab and works on phones, tablets and computers.

What is included

  • A comprehensive, bespoke programme in intimate groups, with regular practice, support and guidance from our team.
  • Termly recognition and awards, with official Swim England certificates and badges all included.
  • Online learner progress tracking via our secure portal.
