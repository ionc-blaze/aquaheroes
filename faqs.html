Frequently asked questions

In a hurry? Here are answers to the questions we hear most.

How much are swimming lessons at Aqua Heroes?

Group lessons start from £12.50 per 25 minute lesson (30 minute slot) in Hornchurch and Ilford. The trial lesson is deducted from term fees.

1:1 lessons start from £35 per 25 minute lesson (30 minute slot). This price is for the trial and then for block booking a term. Pay as you go is £40 per lesson.

1:2 lessons for related learners of a similar ability level start from £40 per 25 minute lesson (30 minute slot), on the same terms. Pay as you go is £45 per lesson.

For prices at your preferred location and time, and any current offers, please contact us.

Where and when are lessons?
  • Hornchurch (The Campion School): Saturday, 3 to 7 pm; Sunday, 7.30 to 11.25 am; Thursday, 8.30 to 10 pm.
  • Brentwood (St Helens Junior School): Wednesday, 4.15 to 6.45 pm.
  • Basildon (James Hornsby School): Monday and Wednesday, 7 to 9 pm; Sunday, 1 to 3 pm.
  • Billericay (Quilters Junior School): Tuesday, 9.30 to 11.30 am; Thursday, 9 am to 2 pm; Friday, 9 am to 6 pm.
  • Loughton (Davenant Foundation School): Thursday, 7.15 to 9.15 pm.
  • Romford (Lime Academy Ravensbourne): Monday and Thursday, 4 to 6 pm.
  • Ilford (Caterham High School): Thursday, 7 to 9 pm.

Teaching slots are allocated by ability level and are subject to change. See all pools and times, submit our booking form to register your space, or contact us to discuss your needs.

Can you teach at my own pool?

Yes. If you have your own indoor pool, we also offer lessons at your home. Please contact us to discuss your preferred location and specific needs.

What age do you teach from?

Our teachers are qualified to teach all age groups. We currently provide lessons for ages 3 months and over, in conjunction with our early years sister school, Little Legends. See our baby and pre-school lessons.

How many children are in each class?

Our regular group lessons have 3 to 4 beginners maximum per teacher, depending on age, and 5 to 6 learners for stages 5 and above, depending on ability. We keep class sizes intimate to ensure the most supportive learning experience.

Which programme do you teach?

Our bespoke lessons, recognition and reward programme is designed around the Swim England Learn to Swim Pathway: stages 1 to 10, and Ducklings for pre-schoolers.

Do you offer a free trial lesson?

For group lessons, the trial lesson is free of charge if you go on to book a 9 week term (T&Cs apply). For 1:1 and 1:2 lessons, the trial is charged at the term block booking price.

Do you teach adults?

Yes. Whether you are a complete beginner or just need some tips to improve your technique, we have the right approach for you. See our adult lessons.

Do you offer lessons for children with SEN?

Yes. We offer SEN 1:1 lessons and SEN group lessons at selected pools. Please contact us to talk through your child's needs. See our private lessons.

Do you run lessons in the school holidays?

Yes. Non-term time crash courses and other sessions are available in half-term holidays, including Christmas, Easter and summer. Price depends on your requirements and is subject to availability.

How do you track progress?

We give termly recognition and awards, with official Swim England certificates and badges all included, and online learner progress tracking through our secure portal.

Who teaches the lessons?

Our qualified, experienced team, led by our founder and lead teacher. We recruit teachers who hold a relevant Level 1 or 2 swimming teacher qualification and a current National Rescue or NPLQ qualification.

How do I book?

Complete the registration form on our booking page. We reply with current availability and any price offers, then arrange a trial lesson at a time that suits you.

Ready to get in the water?

Register for a free, no obligation consultation. We will confirm availability and arrange a trial lesson.
