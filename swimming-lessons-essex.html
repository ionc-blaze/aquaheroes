Swimming lessons across Essex

Find the pool, day and time that suits you, then register and we will confirm what is available.

Aqua Heroes Swim School teaches at seven school pools across Essex and the surrounding areas. Hornchurch, Brentwood, Basildon, Billericay and Loughton have regular lessons, and Romford and Ilford have waiting lists while their pools are refurbished.

Common questions

Where does Aqua Heroes Swim School teach?

We teach at school pools in Hornchurch, Brentwood, Basildon, Billericay and Loughton. Romford and Ilford have waiting lists while their pools are refurbished.

What days and times are lessons?
  • Hornchurch (The Campion School): Saturday, 3 to 7 pm; Sunday, 7.30 to 11.25 am; Thursday, 8.30 to 10 pm.
  • Brentwood (St Helens Junior School): Wednesday, 4.15 to 6.45 pm.
  • Basildon (James Hornsby School): Monday and Wednesday, 7 to 9 pm; Sunday, 1 to 3 pm.
  • Billericay (Quilters Junior School): Tuesday, 9.30 to 11.30 am; Thursday, 9 am to 2 pm; Friday, 9 am to 6 pm.
  • Loughton (Davenant Foundation School): Thursday, 7.15 to 9.15 pm.
  • Romford (Lime Academy Ravensbourne): Monday and Thursday, 4 to 6 pm.
  • Ilford (Caterham High School): Thursday, 7 to 9 pm.
Can you teach at my own pool?

If you have your own indoor pool, we also offer lessons at your home. Contact us to discuss your preferred location and needs.
