Privacy policy

How we use and protect your personal information, and your rights over it.

Aqua Heroes Swim School is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This notice explains how we may use, process and store your personal information, and your rights over it. We may get that information from you, from our partners, through contracts or other legal arrangements, with your permission and consent, or from third parties you have given consent to pass it on to us.

The laws and regulations

The laws that govern personal data in the UK include the Data Protection Act and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The independent authority that upholds information rights in the public interest in the UK is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). More information is at ico.org.uk.

Information we may collect from you

We may collect information because we have a legal reason to (allowed by law or under contract), or because you have consented for a specific purpose. You may give us information by filling in a form or sending us an email. It may be personal, financial, educational or related to another person you have authority to act for. You may also give us information for legal reasons, such as entering a contract with us, buying goods or services from us, or taking a job with Aqua Heroes Swim School.

How we use your information

We may use the information we collect when you register, make a purchase, sign up for our newsletter, respond to a survey or marketing communication, use the website or use certain other site features, for example to follow up after correspondence (Facebook messenger, email or phone enquiries).

Why we collect personal information

  • To provide services to you after you have signed up or booked our swimming lessons or packages.
  • To provide information about products or services you have shown interest in, if you are an existing customer.
  • To provide information about products or services you have purchased, or related ones.
  • To employ you or consider you for employment.
  • To provide goods or services to you under contract.
  • For legal reasons, for example if you have entered into a contract with us.
  • To provide information about our products and services if you have consented to receive it.

Our legal basis for processing

We may process your information because we have a contract with you, you have given us permission, we must provide services to you after you have purchased something, or to comply with the law.

Who we might share your information with

We may share personal information with third parties because you have consented, or for legal reasons. For example, with third parties who provide shipping or publishing services because you have purchased goods or services. If Aqua Heroes Swim School or substantially all of its assets are acquired by a third party, personal data held about customers will be one of the transferred assets. We will also share personal data to comply with a legal obligation, to enforce our website terms of use, legal notice or other agreements, to protect the rights, property or safety of Aqua Heroes Swim School, our customers or others, and to exchange information with other organisations to prevent fraud or reduce credit risk.

We may provide third parties with aggregated or anonymised data with personal information removed. That is not personal data, although you keep your right to object.

Where we store your personal data

We primarily store and process personal data in the EU and European Economic Area (EEA). If we transfer it outside the EEA it will be because you have consented or we have a legal reason to, for example payment processing. If data cannot be processed within the EEA we will comply with all other data protection principles and make sure we have assessed the adequacy of protections.

Passwords

Where we have given you, or you have chosen, a password to access parts of our site, you are responsible for keeping it confidential. Please do not share it with anyone.

Transmission and storage of your personal data

Transmission of information via the internet is not completely secure. We use best practice to encrypt your data to the standard required in the GDPR, but we cannot guarantee the security of data as it is transmitted and stored.

How long we keep your personal information

We will not keep your personal information for longer than required. We keep it for as long as required by law, until we no longer have a valid reason for keeping it, or until you ask us to stop using it. We may keep just enough information to comply with your requests, for example a request to be erased. For our data retention policy, contact info@aquaheroes.co.uk.

If you choose not to give us your personal information

If the information is necessary to supply goods or services under a contract between you and Aqua Heroes Swim School, we will not enter that contract or provide the services or goods without it.

Your rights

  • The right to be informed. This notice keeps you informed about what we do with your personal information.
  • The right to access. Contact info@aquaheroes.co.uk to access the information we hold about you.
  • The right to rectification. If information we hold is inaccurate or incomplete, ask us to correct it. If it was passed to a third party, we must also ask them to correct it.
  • The right to erasure. Also called the right to be forgotten. Ask us to erase your personal data where we have no legal reason to keep it.
  • The right to restrict processing. Ask us to store your data but not process it further. We keep just enough data to respect your request in future.
  • The right to data portability. Obtain and reuse the personal data you provided to us, held by consent or for the performance of a contract, for your own purposes.
  • The right to object. Object to our processing even where it is based on legitimate interests, official authority, direct marketing or statistics.
  • The right to withdraw consent. Withdraw consent at any time and we must stop processing your data.
  • The right to complain. Complain to the ICO if you feel we have not resolved a problem. See ico.org.uk/concerns.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@aquaheroes.co.uk.

Links to other websites

Our website may contain links to and from other websites. Those sites may have their own privacy notices, and we accept no responsibility or liability for them. Please check them before you submit personal data.

Children

Aqua Heroes Swim School does not believe its website or marketing content is appealing to children, nor is it directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable data from anyone under 13. If you are a parent of a child under 13, we will seek your consent if you wish your child to access our content.

Changes to this notice

We may change this notice from time to time. Changes will be posted here and, where appropriate, notified to you in writing. Please check back for updates.
