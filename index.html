Swimming lessons to suit you

Choose a group, a private lesson or a holiday crash course. Every option is taught by our qualified, experienced team.

  • Group lessons

    25 minute lessons with a maximum of 3 to 4 beginners per teacher, depending on age. Stages 5 and above have 5 to 6 learners, depending on ability. The trial lesson is free when you book a 9 week term (T&Cs apply).

    from£12.50per lesson

  • 1:1 and 1:2 lessons

    Including SEN. Tailored to your needs and subject to availability. Prices are lower when you book a 9 week term (T&Cs apply).

    from£351:1, or £40 for 1:2

  • Holiday crash courses

    Half term, Christmas, Easter and summer sessions outside of term time. Pricing depends on what you need and is subject to availability.

    price onrequest

Lessons are available for all ages from 3 months, all year round, except Christmas, Easter and 1 to 2 weeks in the summer while our pools close for planned maintenance. Lessons may be removed at any time, subject to demand.

Welcome to Aqua Heroes

Founded in 2017, Aqua Heroes Swim School provides bespoke, fun and friendly swimming tuition for children and for adults who want to learn as a beginner or improve their technique.

Regular weekly lessons run at hired pools in Essex and the surrounding areas, or we can teach privately at a pool of your choice.
